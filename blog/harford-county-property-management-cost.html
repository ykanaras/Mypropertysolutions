How Much Does Property Management Cost in Harford County, MD?

Compare MY Property Solutions' published Harford County property management fees with worked examples and questions to ask before hiring a manager.

By MY Property Solutions · November 23, 2026

MY Property Solutions offers tenant placement for 90% of one full month's rent, full-service management for an 80% placement fee plus 8% monthly, and management only for 10% monthly when a tenant is already in place. These are our published fees, not a countywide average.

The example assumes a full month's rent of $2,500. Placement fees are based on the full monthly rental rate, not a prorated first payment. Confirm exact billing terms in your agreement.

What does the fee cover?

Placement includes marketing, showings, applicant review, lease support and move-in coordination. Full-service management adds rent collection, quarterly inspections, maintenance coordination and tenant communication. We do not add repair invoice markups; actual repairs remain an ownership expense.

Compare the complete cost, not just the percentage

Ask each company about placement fees, renewal charges, inspection frequency, repair approval thresholds, vacancy costs and how owners receive updates. Keep separate reserves for insurance, taxes, repairs and vacancy. A lower monthly fee is not necessarily a lower total cost.
