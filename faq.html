Frequently asked questions

What areas does MY Property Solutions serve?

We provide residential property management and tenant-placement support throughout Cecil County and Harford County, Maryland, including Elkton, North East, Perryville, Rising Sun, Chesapeake City, Port Deposit, Havre de Grace, Aberdeen, Bel Air, Abingdon, Belcamp, Edgewood, Joppa, and nearby communities.

Does MY Property Solutions offer tenant placement without ongoing management?

Yes. Tenant Placement Only is available for owners who want help marketing the property, coordinating showings, reviewing applicants, and placing a tenant without ongoing monthly management. See our service options and pricing.

Can you manage a rental that already has a tenant?

Yes. Property Management Only is available for owners who already have a tenant in place and want ongoing management support.

What is included with full-service property management?

Full-service management includes tenant placement plus ongoing rent collection, maintenance coordination, inspections, lease enforcement, renewals, and tenant communication.

How does tenant screening work?

Applications are reviewed as a complete profile, including credit history, income, employment, rental history, background information, and overall qualification rather than relying on a single number alone.

Do you coordinate rental-property maintenance?

Yes. Maintenance coordination is part of full-service management. MY Property Solutions does not add a markup to maintenance or repair invoices.

How can I see current rentals?

Visit our Available Properties page for current rental information. Our website links directly to Rentec for current rental listings.

How do I get started as a property owner?

Visit the Work With Us page to compare service options and pricing, then contact MY Property Solutions with information about your property and the level of support you need.
