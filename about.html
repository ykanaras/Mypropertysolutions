About MY Property Solutions

MY Property Solutions is a locally operated residential property management company serving rental-property owners and residents throughout Cecil County and Harford County, Maryland.

Talk With Us About Your PropertyView Services & Pricing

Local property management with hands-on service

Led by Michelle and Yianni, MY Property Solutions focuses on practical, responsive support for local rental-property owners. Our services include tenant placement, full-service property management, and ongoing management for properties that already have a tenant in place.

For Property Owners

Rental marketing, showing coordination, applicant screening, lease support, rent collection, maintenance coordination, inspections, renewals, and day-to-day tenant communication.

Local Focus

We concentrate on Cecil County and Harford County so owners have a team familiar with local communities, rental demand, property types, and leasing activity.

Straightforward Service

We emphasize clear communication, transparent service options, careful applicant review, and practical oversight of the rental relationship.

Where we work

Our service area includes communities such as Elkton, North East, Perryville, Rising Sun, Chesapeake City, Port Deposit, Havre de Grace, Aberdeen, Bel Air, Abingdon, Belcamp, Edgewood, Joppa, and surrounding areas.
