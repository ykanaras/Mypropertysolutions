Tenant Placement

Property management questions in Perryville

What should I prepare before listing my Perryville home?

Start with the home's condition, a clear list of included appliances and services, and a plan for yard care and utilities. For a home like our Frenchtown Road rental, outdoor upkeep and the screened-in porch are useful details to address before showings. We can review your property and recommend a listing plan.

Will my Perryville home rent for $2,200 per month?

The $2,200 figure is the result for 1518 Frenchtown Road, not a town-wide rent estimate. Your home's size, condition, features, included services, and competing listings will shape its rental potential.

Can you find a tenant for my Perryville home without ongoing management?

Yes. Tenant Placement Only covers rental marketing, showing coordination, applicant screening, lease support, and move-in coordination. The fee is 90% of the first full month's rent. Full-service and management-only options are also available.
