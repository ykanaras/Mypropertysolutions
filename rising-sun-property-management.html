Local rental management for Rising Sun property owners

Renting out a home in Rising Sun starts with a clear picture of its space, condition, and location. We help owners explain those details in the listing, personally coordinate showings, review applicants, and choose the level of ongoing management that fits their needs.

Tenant Placement

Rental marketing, inquiry and showing coordination, applicant screening, lease support, and move-in coordination. Tenant Placement Only is 90% of the first full month's rent.

Full-Service Management

Tenant placement plus rent collection, maintenance coordination, inspections, lease enforcement, renewals, and tenant communication. Full-Service Management includes an 80% placement fee based on the first full month's rent, plus 8% of gross monthly rent for ongoing management.

Management for Existing Tenants

Owners who already have a tenant can use ongoing management for rent collection, maintenance coordination, inspections, lease administration, and communication. Management Only is 10% of monthly rent.

Property management questions in Rising Sun

How do you market a Rising Sun rental with unusual features?

We describe the feature accurately and show it in the listing photos. For example, basement access through a garage-style door should be explained as walkout access unless vehicle access has been confirmed. Clear descriptions help set expectations before a showing.

How do you estimate rent for my Rising Sun home?

We review the property's bedrooms, bathrooms, condition, usable space, features, and competing rentals. Our Pearl Street lease at $2,375 per month is one local example; it is not a guaranteed rent for another home.

Can you find a tenant for my Rising Sun home without ongoing management?

Yes. Tenant Placement Only covers rental marketing, showing coordination, applicant screening, lease support, and move-in coordination. The fee is 90% of the first full month's rent. Full-service and management-only options are also available.
